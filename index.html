How the numbers are built
  • All-in cost = retail × 1.08 tax + shipping when under the retailer's free threshold (Target and Best Buy $5.99 under $35, Walmart $6.99 under $35, Pokémon Center $7.95 under $20) + Obey fee (the real drop fee where one is scheduled, else 5% of market, $1–$30).
  • Market = TCGplayer market price for the matched product. Pokémon Center ETBs are priced as the PC-stamped exclusive versions.
  • Strength: strong under 50% of market, solid 50–60%, fringe 60–70%, fails 70%+.
  • Retail source "MSRP default" means no price on the dashboard label and none at another retailer, so a standard MSRP was assumed.
  • "Stuck" = the site accepts a new check but ignores an uncheck for that item (Pokémon Center only).
